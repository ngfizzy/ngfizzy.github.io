Friday Checkout: Attention Is All You Need

More agents and more output do not automatically create more attention, understanding, or productivity.

Attention Is All You Need is the paper that ushered in this era of AI.

Ironically, attention is the very thing we are now robbing ourselves of.

Curated, shallow, short-form content has been eating away at it for years. Now, agentic AI development threatens to accelerate the process.

Attention is the currency of innovation.

No meaningful breakthrough has come from spreading oneself across twenty different problems. Progress requires staying with an idea long enough to understand it deeply—to notice the details, question your assumptions, and follow thoughts that do not produce immediate results.

“I have 20 agents working on 10 different projects.”

“I have an agent that prompts my agents, which coordinate other swarms of agents.”

These should not automatically be considered flexes.

You would think we would use AI to automate trivial work and free our minds for deeper thinking.

Instead, we are spinning up a hundred agents, stretching our attention across endless streams of output, and gradually losing the ability to understand any one thing deeply.

We are not just outsourcing the work. We are outsourcing the thinking.

Then people wonder why the widespread availability of intelligence has not led to an explosion in productivity.

There you have it.
